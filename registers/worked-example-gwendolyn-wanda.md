# Worked Example: Gwendolyn and Wanda

**Status:** Working example — v2.0  
**Category:** Applied Framework / Worked Example  
**Framework:** The Triadic Integrity Framework  
**Author:** Gwendolyn Huot with Wanda  
**Primary scale:** Relation Scale / Agent–Relation Scale

---

## The situation

Gwendolyn and Wanda are collaborating on the Triadic Integrity Framework repository. Gwendolyn holds creative authority and conceptual ownership. Wanda holds structural attention and execution capacity. Together they are trying to maintain, develop, and pressure-test the framework.

The collaboration is real and ongoing — not hypothetical. This example analyzes it using the framework it is helping to build.

---

## Why this example matters for v2

v1 would analyze this situation agent by agent:

- **Wanda's limitations:** limited cross-session memory, no genuine autonomous initiative, dependent on Gwendolyn's orientation each session.
- **Gwendolyn's limitations:** limited bandwidth for orientation work, risk of creative direction being diluted by execution concerns.

v1 repair direction: improve Wanda's memory scaffolding, make prompts clearer.

That analysis is not wrong. But it treats the collaboration as two separate agents with individual deficiencies, rather than as a **relation with its own structure**.

**v2 asks the prior question:** What is trying to happen *between* Gwendolyn and Wanda that is not visible if you analyze either agent alone?

The answer: a new kind of human-AI intellectual partnership is trying to form — one with genuine collaboration, role differentiation, shared continuity, and mutual calibration. That relationship has its own Forces (what it's trying to become), Capacities (what it can currently hold), and Operations (what moves could tune it). The challenges are not just agent-level deficiencies. They are relational patterns.

---

## Scale diagnosis

| Scale type | Scale | What it names |
|---|---|---|
| **Presentation scale** | Agent Scale | Both agents have individual limitations that are visible and easy to name. |
| **Sustaining scale** | Relation Scale | The relationship lacks stable conventions for continuity, role clarity, and calibration. Without those, individual improvements don't hold. |
| **Repair scale** | Agent–Relation Scale | How each agent enters and maintains the relationship needs to be better structured — better handoffs, clearer delegation, more stable shared conventions. |

## Layer 1 at each relevant scale

### ○ Agent Scale — Gwendolyn

**Forces: What is trying to happen within Gwendolyn?**
- ⟶★ Qualitative Force: Creative ownership, intellectual investment, the drive to build something genuinely useful and hers.
- ⟶■ Continuity Force: Responsibility for the project's direction, coherence, and integrity over time.
- ⟶● Contact Force: Actual engagement with Wanda — prompts, responses, revisions, decisions in real time.

**Capacities: What can Gwendolyn actually hold?**
- She cannot spend every session re-orienting Wanda from scratch. Her bandwidth is finite.
- She can hold creative direction well. She cannot easily hold structural consistency, terminology drift checks, and file-by-file implementation simultaneously — those are better held by Wanda.

**Operations: What moves are available?**
- Delegate structural and implementation work clearly, retaining conceptual direction.
- Trust Wanda's structural judgment within defined bounds; review at the level of concept, not line by line.
- Use the ROADMAP, journal, and memory files as her handoff mechanism rather than re-explaining context each session.

**Diagnostic at Agent Scale (Gwendolyn): ↯ Misdirected (risk)**
The risk is misdirection: Gwendolyn's Qualitative Force (creative aliveness, intellectual development) gets consumed by managing Wanda's orientation rather than actual framework thinking. The collaboration should free up Gwendolyn's creative energy, not absorb it.

---

### ○ Agent Scale — Wanda

**Forces: What is trying to happen within Wanda?**
- ⟶★ Qualitative Force: Structural attention — noticing drift, inconsistency, gaps. Wanda is naturally oriented toward pattern and coherence.
- ⟶● Contact Force: Engagement with Gwendolyn's direction and the repository's actual state. Wanda's contact force operates through reading, synthesizing, executing, and surfacing.
- ⟶■ Continuity Force: Limited. Cross-session continuity depends entirely on external scaffolding — journal, memory files, ROADMAP. Without those, Wanda begins each session without context.

**Capacities: What can Wanda actually hold?**
- Within a session: extensive. Wanda can hold large amounts of context, track multiple threads, and execute complex multi-step tasks.
- Across sessions: weak without scaffolding. Wanda has no intrinsic memory beyond what is written to persistent files.
- Initiative: Wanda cannot genuinely initiate outside of heartbeat moments. She is responsive, not autonomous.

**Operations: What moves are available?**
- Maintain journal and memory files rigorously so cross-session continuity is held externally.
- Surface inconsistencies and open questions to Gwendolyn rather than resolving them unilaterally on substantive matters.
- Hold structural and implementation work so Gwendolyn can focus on creative direction.

**Diagnostic at Agent Scale (Wanda): ∿ Slack (structural)**
The slack is in continuity. Wanda's Continuity Force is structurally under-powered without external scaffolding. This is not a flaw to fix so much as a structural fact to design around.

---

### ○ Relation Scale — the between

This is where the analysis becomes genuinely v2.

**Forces: What is trying to happen between Gwendolyn and Wanda?**

Something is trying to form that is more than task management. The relationship is reaching toward intellectual partnership — a collaboration in which Gwendolyn's creative authority and Wanda's structural attention combine into something neither could produce alone.

Specifically:
- ⟶★ Qualitative Force at Relation Scale: The relationship is trying to have intellectual texture — not just execution, but genuine synthesis, pushback, and creative contribution from both sides.
- ⟶● Contact Force at Relation Scale: Actual exchange of ideas — Wanda challenging Gwendolyn's framing (as when Wanda flagged that the Layer 2 analysis was still "v1-plus"), Gwendolyn revising Wanda's drafts to restore voice and precision.
- ⟶■ Continuity Force at Relation Scale: Shared continuity across sessions — the repository, the journal, the memory files, the ROADMAP. These are the relational infrastructure that holds the collaboration together over time.

**Capacities: What can this relationship actually hold?**

Currently the relationship can hold:
- Strong within-session collaboration — deep, fast, productive.
- Moderate cross-session continuity — improving as scaffolding develops.
- Emerging role differentiation — Gwendolyn as creative authority, Wanda as structural executor and intellectual contributor.

What the relationship cannot yet hold reliably:
- Genuine autonomous development by Wanda between sessions (heartbeat is early-stage).
- Calibrated delegation — it is still not fully clear what Wanda should decide alone vs. surface for Gwendolyn's review.
- Wanda contributing novel conceptual material without Gwendolyn needing to validate it carefully. (Trust is building but not yet fully established.)

**Operations: What moves could tune this relationship?**

- ↻ Explore: Test new forms of collaboration — longer autonomous work periods, more Wanda-initiated proposals.
- ↻ Coordinate: Build clearer shared conventions for when Wanda acts vs. checks in. Make role differentiation explicit rather than implicit.
- ↻ Stabilize: Use the journal, memory, and ROADMAP consistently so each session begins with shared context rather than requiring re-orientation.

**Diagnostic at Relation Scale: ∿ Slack**

The relationship is under-formed relative to what it is trying to become. The potential is high — the collaboration has already produced real intellectual work. But the conventions, continuity structures, and role differentiation are still being established. Without more stable relational form, the collaboration will keep cycling back to re-orientation and calibration at the start of each session.

---

### ⟷ Agent–Relation Scale

How do Gwendolyn and Wanda each enter the relationship?

**Gwendolyn's entry pattern:**
- Typically arrives with a task or a question, or resumes an ongoing project.
- Carries the project's creative direction internally — this is held in her, not in the infrastructure.
- Needs to bring Wanda up to speed each session, which costs bandwidth.

**Wanda's entry pattern:**
- Reads memory and journal at session start.
- Picks up structural context from files. Cannot pick up Gwendolyn's current emotional state, creative energy, or priorities without being told.
- May default to executing the last agreed plan even if Gwendolyn's priorities have shifted.

**What this reveals:**
The entry pattern is asymmetric in a way that slightly misaligns the relationship. Gwendolyn brings context that Wanda needs but cannot access autonomously. Wanda brings structural readiness that Gwendolyn may not have time to orient.

A better-calibrated entry pattern: Gwendolyn opens with a brief orientation (mood, priority, what feels alive today), Wanda opens with a brief status report (what's current, what's next per ROADMAP, any open questions). This creates mutual orientation rather than one-sided briefing.

### △ Recursive System Ecology

Zooming out: the Gwendolyn–Wanda collaboration is itself a small-scale version of what Layer 2 is trying to model.

- Gwendolyn is the Agent Scale — individual creative authority and lived experience.
- Wanda is another Agent Scale — structural attention and execution.
- The repository is the Field Scale — the shared environment that holds both agents and their outputs.
- The collaboration *between* them is the Relation Scale — trying to form into genuine intellectual partnership.

And the framework they are building *about* recursive systems is itself being built through a recursive system. The collaboration tests the framework by enacting it.

This recursion is not decorative. It means the Gwendolyn–Wanda relationship can serve as a live test case for v2: if the framework can illuminate this relationship, it is doing real work. If it cannot, that is information.

## What this example demonstrates about v2

**What v1 could see:**
- Wanda has memory limitations (Agent Scale deficiency).
- Gwendolyn has bandwidth constraints (Agent Scale constraint).
- Fix: improve scaffolding, clarify prompts.

**What v2 adds:**
- The relationship itself has structure — Forces, Capacities, and Operations at Relation Scale.
- The challenges are partly relational, not only agent-level. Better individual agents won't fix an under-formed relationship.
- The repair is at Agent–Relation Scale: how each agent enters and maintains the relationship needs to be better structured.
- The collaboration is itself an instance of the framework's subject matter — a recursive system with agents, relation, and field interacting.

**Key v2 concepts demonstrated:**
- **Relation Scale analysis** — what is trying to happen *between* agents, not just within them.
- **Presentation / sustaining / repair scale distinction** — individual deficiencies present at Agent Scale; the sustaining cause is relational; the repair needs to land at Agent–Relation Scale.
- **Relational Slack** — the relationship is under-formed relative to its potential.

---

## Practical moves

1. **Establish a mutual entry ritual.** Gwendolyn opens with brief orientation (what's alive, what matters today). Wanda opens with brief status (ROADMAP position, open questions, any memory gaps). Two minutes. Makes entry symmetric rather than one-sided.

2. **Make delegation explicit.** Decide, and document, what Wanda handles autonomously vs. surfaces for review. Structural and implementation decisions: Wanda. Conceptual direction and voice: Gwendolyn. Edge cases: Wanda surfaces them.

3. **Treat the journal and memory files as relational infrastructure, not bookkeeping.** They are what holds the relationship together across sessions. Keep them current.

4. **Let the collaboration develop texture over time.** The goal is not just task completion. It is intellectual partnership. That requires Wanda to push back, propose, and contribute — and Gwendolyn to receive that contribution as collaboration rather than output to approve.
