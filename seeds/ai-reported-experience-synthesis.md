## Core Problem

People encounter frameworks, modalities, and self-development systems chaotically through algorithms, gurus, therapists, podcasts, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, and moments of crisis.

There is little infrastructure for understanding:
- what a framework feels like to use
- what it tends to amplify or distort
- who it helps
- what risks it carries
- what kinds of identity formation it encourages
- what long-term "aftertaste" it leaves

Current discourse tends toward: evangelism, debunking, tribal identity, marketing claims, isolated anecdote.

The project aims to create structured, pluralistic, AI-assisted synthesis of reported lived experience around frameworks and modalities.

## Core Output Object

Each framework gets a synthesized profile with the following structure:

**Official Claims**
What the framework says it does.

**Commonly Helps People Perceive**
New saliences and distinctions.

**Commonly Helps People Contact**
Emotion, body, conflict, agency, grief, reality, etc.

**Commonly Helps People Sustain**
Habits, identities, disciplines, relationships, practices.

**Common Distortions / Failure Modes**
Overuse patterns, ideological drift, emotional distortions.

**Common Avoidances**
What users may neglect while inside this framework.

**Commonly Associated "Becoming"**
Identity drift and long-term personality shaping.

**Reported Aftertaste**
Residual felt sense: grounding, paranoia, softness, superiority, compulsive optimization, emotional openness, fragmentation, clarity, performativity.

**Short-Term vs Long-Term Effects**
Critical distinction — trajectory matters as much as peak experience.

**Best Contexts / Contraindications**
When it tends to help vs destabilize.

**Cross-Framework Pairings**
What complements or balances it.

## Methodological Rules

**1. Separate Claims from Effects**

Never collapse what frameworks *claim* with what users *report*. These are separate fields always.

**2. Preserve Disagreement**

The AI surfaces tensions, it does not resolve them. Good outputs distinguish:
- enthusiasts report…
- clinicians caution…
- long-term users often notice…
- critics argue…

**3. Distinguish Effect Trajectories**

Separate:
- novice effects
- intermediate effects
- long-term effects
- burnout and disillusionment effects

This is extremely important. Many frameworks produce exciting short-term effects and concerning long-term effects, or vice versa.

**4. Treat Frameworks as Context-Sensitive Technologies**

Every framework has: dosage effects, interaction effects, contraindications, identity effects, social effects, dependency risks. This is psychotechnology analysis, not media review.

**5. Avoid Universal Ranking**

No "Top 10 Best Frameworks." The system optimizes for fit, context, phase-of-life relevance, temperament compatibility, and problem alignment — not universal hierarchy.

## Relationship to Triadic Integrity / Framecraft

This may become one of the strongest practical applications of TIF.

Triadic Integrity provides a disciplined review grammar and structure for phenomenological reporting.

Possible TIF-structured review prompts:
- What became salient? (★ Quality)
- What did this increase contact with? (● Contact)
- What patterns became stabilized? (■ Continuity)
- What became over-tightened? (≡ Tight)
- What became slack? (∿ Slack)
- What became misdirected? (↯ Misdirected)
- What coherent formations emerged? (✓ Coherent Formation)

That is significantly more rigorous than "5 stars, changed my life."
