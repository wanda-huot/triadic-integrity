# Triad Taxonomy

*Seed concept — early stage, worth not losing.*
*Originated in conversation with Wanda, May 2026.*

---

## The Core Provocation

Not all triads are secretly the same. The number three gets used in structurally different ways — and most people never notice the difference.

The interesting question is not: *why do triads keep appearing?*

It is: *what job is the triangle doing?*

Is it naming scarcity? Creating balance? Marking sequence? Distributing roles? Producing emergence? Sanctifying completeness?

That question becomes the basis for a typology of triadic functions.

---

## A First Sorting Scheme

### Tradeoff Triads
You cannot maximize all three at once. The tension is real and structural.

*Examples: cheap / fast / good. Speed / quality / cost.*

The triangle names a constraint space. Choosing more of one means less of another. The form reveals scarcity.

---

### Generative Triads
Three elements co-produce a whole. No single element is sufficient; together they generate something that none of them contains alone.

*Examples: Quality / Contact / Continuity (Triadic Integrity). Thesis / antithesis / synthesis — when the synthesis is genuinely emergent, not just a compromise.*

The triangle is not a tradeoff. It is an ecology. Health is coherence, not balance-seeking.

---

### Developmental Triads
A movement unfolds through three phases. The form is sequential, not simultaneous.

*Examples: thesis / antithesis / synthesis. Beginning / middle / end. Setup / complication / resolution.*

The shape marks progression or transformation across time. The third term is not a synthesis of the first two — it is an arrival.

Note: this structure is often borrowed cosmetically. Not every three-stage model involves genuine development. Watch for flatness masquerading as sequence.

---

### Role Triads
Three positions form a recurring social or relational pattern. The positions are defined by their relation to each other.

*Examples: Karpman Drama Triangle (victim / rescuer / persecutor). Parent / child / mediator. Sender / receiver / medium.*

The triangle is a map of social dynamics, not of elements or phases. The insight is that certain role configurations reproduce themselves across wildly different contexts.

---

### Mediating Triads
A third term holds the relation between two. Without the mediator, the two poles cannot connect.

*Examples: Peirce's sign / object / interpretant. Speaker / meaning / listener. Artist / work / audience.*

The triangle is not symmetric. The third term is qualitatively different — it is the relational hinge. Peircean semiotics is the richest development of this form.

---

### Sacred or Symbolic Triads
Three is used to express completeness, mystery, divinity, or cosmic pattern. The threeness is not explained; it is asserted or experienced as self-evident.

*Examples: The Trinity (Father / Son / Holy Spirit). Body / mind / spirit. Heaven / earth / human. Past / present / future.*

These triads are not falsifiable. They are not meant to be. They operate in the register of orientation, not analysis. The question is not "is this structurally sound?" but "what does this allow people to dwell in?"

## The Work Ahead

A richer taxonomy would:

- Clarify what distinguishes these types at the structural level, not just by example
- Identify hybrid triads that operate across types simultaneously
- Ask what each type is good for and what each type tends to distort
- Connect the taxonomy to the Mental Geometry project (see: seeds/mental-geometry-guide.md)
- Ask whether Triadic Integrity is best understood as generative, mediating, or something more specific

This is not a completed atlas. It is a first sorting attempt — a question worth developing.
