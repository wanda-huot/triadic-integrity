# triadic-integrity

**The Triadic Integrity Framework** — also known as **Tri Everything** — is an AI-native, recursive micro-grammar of experience.

It is inspired by Charles Sanders Peirce’s categories of **Firstness**, **Secondness**, and **Thirdness**, and begins from three structural primitives:

- **★ Quality**
- **● Contact**
- **■ Continuity**

The framework offers a simple metaphysical and mechanical pattern for noticing what is happening, understanding stuckness, and choosing a better next move.

Made by **Gwendolyn Huot with ChatGPT**.

---

## Short description

The Triadic Integrity Framework is a recursive micro-grammar of experience.

It describes how **★ Quality**, **● Contact**, and **■ Continuity** form patterns across experience, action, interpretation, and practical decision-making.

The framework is designed to be usable by both humans and AI systems.

It can help people and AI agents:

- describe what is happening
- identify stuck patterns
- distinguish different kinds of distortion
- choose better next moves
- translate abstract structure into practical language
- compare different human-facing frameworks, practices, and registers

---

## Core idea

The framework begins with a simple structural pattern:

```text
★ Quality + ● Contact + ■ Continuity → ★–●–■ Triadic Integration
```

In plain language:

- **★ Quality** is felt salience, vividness, tone, or character.
- **● Contact** is encounter, friction, resistance, relation, or reality-contact.
- **■ Continuity** is pattern, persistence, form, habit, or recognizability over time.

These three primitives generate three dyads:

- **★–● Quality–Contact**
- **●–■ Contact–Continuity**
- **★–■ Quality–Continuity**

The dyads generate a relational field, and that field can produce **★–●–■ Triadic Integration**: the coherent appearance of a formed triadic relation.

## The Three Articulation Layers

The framework is organized into **The Three Articulation Layers**.

```text
Layer 0 → Layer 1 → Layer 2
structure → mechanism → human language
```

### Layer 0: The Structural Layer

Layer 0 defines the basic metaphysical grammar:

- **★ Quality**
- **● Contact**
- **■ Continuity**
- the three dyads
- **★–●–■ Triadic Integration**

Layer 0 is structural, descriptive, and non-diagnostic.

Start here:

```text
framework/layer-0-structural-layer.md
```

---

### Layer 1: The Mechanical Layer

Layer 1 translates the Layer 0 structure into three mechanical systems:

- **⟶ The Three Forces**
- **⬡ The Three Capacities**
- **↻ The Three Operations**

The preferred summary is:

> **⟶ Forces generate movement. ⬡ Capacities determine what the system can bear. ↻ Operations tune the system toward responsive integrity.**

Layer 1 is where the framework becomes operational. It supports practical diagnosis, decision-making, and AI-assisted reasoning.

Start here:

```text
framework/layer-1-mechanical-layer.md
```

---

### Layer 2: The Human Registers Layer

Layer 2 translates the canonical structure into usable human language.

A **Human Register** is not a separate framework. It is a translation layer: a way of speaking the same underlying structure for a particular audience, domain, or situation.

Examples include:

- **The Three Pulls Register**
- **The Kid Register**
- **The Working Register**
- **The Embodied Register**
- **The Christian Theological Register**

Start here:

```text
framework/layer-2-human-registers.md
```

## Important note on The Three Pulls

**The Three Pulls** is the early, original, and formative language of this project.

The framework first became visible through the language of:

- Aliveness
- Coordination / Encounter
- Stability / Steadiness
- the Wholesome Triangle

Later, the framework was refactored from **Layer 0** with AI assistance. That refactor produced the more objective Layer 1 language of:

- **⟶ The Three Forces**
- **⬡ The Three Capacities**
- **↻ The Three Operations**

This means **The Three Pulls** are not discarded.

They are preserved as a historically important and practically useful **Layer 2 Human Register**.

Use:

- **⟶ The Three Forces** for canonical Layer 1 structure
- **The Three Pulls** for human-facing practical reflection

## Diagnostic overlay

The framework uses three diagnostic distortion patterns:

- **∿ Slack**
- **≡ Tight**
- **↯ Misdirected**

And one well-formed condition:

- **✓ Coherent Formation**

Important distinction:

```text
∿ Slack, ≡ Tight, and ↯ Misdirected are distortions.
✓ Coherent Formation is the well-formed condition.
```

**✓ Coherent Formation** is not a fourth distortion.

It means the relevant parts are functioning together with enough responsive integrity to proceed.

## AI-native use

This repository is intentionally written for both human readers and AI systems.

The framework can be used by AI agents to:

- parse a situation structurally
- identify forces, capacities, and operations
- detect Slack, Tight, or Misdirected patterns
- suggest what Coherent Formation might require
- choose an appropriate Human Register
- provide practical next-step guidance
- compare other frameworks by function

A basic AI reasoning pattern might be:

```text
1. What is salient?                  → ★ Quality
2. What is being encountered?        → ● Contact
3. What pattern is continuing?       → ■ Continuity
4. What force is active?             → ⟶ The Three Forces
5. What can the system bear?         → ⬡ The Three Capacities
6. What move is needed?              → ↻ The Three Operations
7. Is the pattern Slack, Tight, or Misdirected?
8. What would Coherent Formation look like?
9. Which Human Register would make this useful?
```

The framework should support reasoning without making the AI sound like an oracle.

The goal is not mystification.

The goal is clearer interpretation and better next moves.

## What this framework is not

The Triadic Integrity Framework is not:

- a clinical diagnostic tool
- a therapy modality
- a medical system
- a religious doctrine
- a finished philosophy
- a personality typology
- a claim to explain everything
- a strict scholarly interpretation of Peirce

It is a practical metaphysical and mechanical grammar.

It may help organize reflection, decision-making, self-understanding, AI reasoning, and comparison across frameworks.
