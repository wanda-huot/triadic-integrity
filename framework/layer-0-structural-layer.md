## 1. Purpose

Layer 0 is the structural foundation of The Triadic Integrity Framework.

It names the most basic pattern underneath the framework:

```text
three primitives → three dyads → relational field → triadic integration
```

Layer 0 is not yet about psychology, therapy, productivity, relationships, or practical decision-making.

It is the abstract structural grammar that later becomes usable through:

- **Layer 1: The Mechanical Layer**
- **Layer 2: Recursive System Scales**
- **Applied Framework** materials such as Registers, Bridges, Expression Formats, and Tools

Layer 0 asks:

```text
What are the basic structural ingredients of a lived moment?
```

The current answer is:

- **★ Quality**
- **● Contact**
- **■ Continuity**

These three primitives generate three dyads.

The dyads generate the possibility of **★–●–■ Triadic Integration**.

---

## 2. Core claim

The core claim of Layer 0 is:

> Experience can be described as a recurring triadic pattern involving felt salience, encounter with reality, and continuing form.

In the framework’s terms:

- **★ Quality** names felt salience, vividness, tone, or character.
- **● Contact** names encounter, friction, reality-contact, resistance, or relation.
- **■ Continuity** names pattern, persistence, form, habit, or recognizability over time.

These are not three separate things.

They are three distinguishable aspects of how experience becomes intelligible.

---

## 3. The three primitives

Layer 0 begins with three primitives.

A primitive is a basic structural term. It is not derived from another term inside the framework.

| Symbol | Name | Short definition | Variation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **★** | **Quality** | Felt salience, vividness, tone, or character. | Varies in intensity and character. |
| **●** | **Contact** | Encounter, friction, reality-contact, or relation. | Varies in degree and kind. |
| **■** | **Continuity** | Pattern, persistence, form, habit, or recognizability. | Varies in stability and pattern. |

---

## 4. ★ Quality

**★ Quality** is felt salience.

It is the vividness, tone, character, or “what-it-is-like” of experience before it has been fully interpreted, acted on, or stabilized into a pattern.

It includes the sense that something matters, glows, attracts attention, feels charged, feels dull, feels beautiful, feels strange, feels alive, feels off, or otherwise has a particular qualitative character.

### Formal description

**★ Quality varies in intensity and character.**

### Examples

- a color feeling vivid
- a memory having a particular emotional tone
- a room feeling warm, tense, stale, or alive
- an idea seeming interesting before it is fully explained
- a phrase having a felt rightness or wrongness

### Notes

**★ Quality is not the same as desire.**

Desire may arise from Quality, but Quality is more basic. It is the felt character or salience of something before it becomes a clear want, plan, judgment, or action.

---

## 5. ● Contact

**● Contact** is encounter.

It is the point at which something meets reality, resistance, friction, otherness, embodiment, consequence, or relation.

Contact includes the fact that experience is not just private inward feeling. Something is encountered. Something pushes back. Something responds. Something must be dealt with.

### Formal description

**● Contact varies in degree and kind.**

### Examples

- touching a physical object
- having a conversation with another person
- meeting resistance while trying to do a task
- discovering that an idea does or does not work in practice
- being interrupted by reality
- receiving feedback from another person or system

### Notes

**● Contact is not merely social harmony.**

It includes encounter in a broad sense: friction, resistance, interaction, reality-testing, collision, response, and relation with what is not simply under one’s control.

---

## 6. ■ Continuity

**■ Continuity** is pattern over time.

It is persistence, recognizability, form, habit, memory, structure, repetition, or the ability of something to hold together across moments.

Without Continuity, experience may be vivid and interactive, but it does not stabilize into a recognizable shape.

### Formal description

**■ Continuity varies in stability and pattern.**

### Examples

- a habit repeating
- a story retaining its theme
- a relationship having a recognizable pattern
- a practice becoming stable over time
- a concept becoming reusable
- a life having continuity of identity, memory, or form

### Notes

**■ Continuity is not the same as rigidity or control.**

Continuity can be flexible. A living pattern can persist while adapting. In this framework, Continuity names the possibility of recognizable form, not the demand for fixed sameness.

---

## 7. The three dyads

The three primitives generate three dyads.

A dyad is a two-term relation. It is not yet the full triadic structure, but it is more than an isolated primitive.

| Symbol | Name | Short definition | Variation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **★–●** | **Quality–Contact** | Felt salience meeting encounter. | Varies in the vividness, specificity, and felt tone of encounter. |
| **●–■** | **Contact–Continuity** | Encounter becoming repeatable relation. | Varies in the duration, repeatability, and reliability of relation. |
| **★–■** | **Quality–Continuity** | Felt salience persisting as recognizable form. | Varies in the retention, recognizability, and persistence of salience. |

The dyads matter because the framework does not move directly from three isolated primitives to a finished whole.

The sequence is:

```text
three primitives → three dyads → relational field → triadic integration
```

---

## 8. ★–● Quality–Contact

**★–● Quality–Contact** is the relation between felt salience and encounter.

It describes what happens when something not only has a qualitative character, but also comes into contact with reality, resistance, interaction, or specificity.

### Useful question

```text
Where is felt salience meeting reality?
```

### Formal description

**★–● Quality–Contact varies in the vividness, specificity, and felt tone of encounter.**

### Examples

- a vague interest becoming vivid when tested in conversation
- a beautiful idea meeting practical resistance
- a feeling becoming sharper when expressed to another person
- a possibility becoming more real when acted on
- a sensation becoming meaningful through direct attention

### Notes

Without **● Contact**, **★ Quality** can remain private, atmospheric, or untested.

Without **★ Quality**, **● Contact** can become bare collision or mechanical interaction.

Together, they create vivid encounter.

---

## 9. ●–■ Contact–Continuity

**●–■ Contact–Continuity** is the relation between encounter and pattern.

It describes what happens when contact with reality becomes repeatable, reliable, memorable, or structurally consequential.

### Useful question

```text
What contact is becoming a pattern?
```

### Formal description

**●–■ Contact–Continuity varies in the duration, repeatability, and reliability of relation.**

### Examples

- repeated practice forming skill
- a conversation becoming a relationship
- a one-time action becoming a habit
- feedback becoming a stable adjustment
- repeated friction revealing a pattern

### Notes

Without **■ Continuity**, **● Contact** can remain episodic or disconnected.

Without **● Contact**, **■ Continuity** can become abstract, stale, or detached from reality.

Together, they create reliable relation.

---

## 10. ★–■ Quality–Continuity

**★–■ Quality–Continuity** is the relation between felt salience and continuing form.

It describes what happens when something vivid, meaningful, beautiful, charged, or interesting persists across time and becomes recognizable.

### Useful question

```text
What keeps mattering over time?
```

### Formal description

**★–■ Quality–Continuity varies in the retention, recognizability, and persistence of salience.**

### Examples

- a melody staying recognizable across variations
- an idea retaining its felt importance over time
- a personal style becoming identifiable
- a memory preserving its emotional tone
- a value continuing to matter across changing situations

### Notes

Without **■ Continuity**, **★ Quality** may flare and disappear.

Without **★ Quality**, **■ Continuity** may persist without felt significance.

Together, they create recognizable salience.

---

## 11. ★–●–■ Triadic Integration

**★–●–■ Triadic Integration** is the emergent coherence generated by the three dyads.

It is not a fourth primitive.

It is the formed relational field that becomes possible when:

- **★ Quality** gives felt salience
- **● Contact** brings encounter with reality
- **■ Continuity** provides pattern and persistence
- **★–● Quality–Contact** makes encounter vivid
- **●–■ Contact–Continuity** makes relation reliable
- **★–■ Quality–Continuity** makes salience recognizable

Together, these relations produce a coherent triadic whole.

### Formal description

**★–●–■ Triadic Integration varies in the coherence, complexity, and legibility of appearance.**

### Important distinction

**★–●–■ Triadic Integration is not a moral condition.**

It is not the same as health, virtue, success, happiness, or psychological wellness.

It is a structural description of a formed triadic relation.

Later layers may interpret coherent or distorted formations in practical, diagnostic, therapeutic, creative, relational, or spiritual language. But Layer 0 itself remains structural and descriptive.

```text

                /           \

              /               \
             /                 \
    ● Contact ---------------- ■ Continuity
```

The three vertices are the primitives:

- **★ Quality**
- **● Contact**
- **■ Continuity**

The three sides are the dyads:

- **★–● Quality–Contact**
- **●–■ Contact–Continuity**
- **★–■ Quality–Continuity**

The center is the emergent relational field:

- **★–●–■ Triadic Integration**

The visual grammar is:

```text
three primitives → three dyads → relational field → emergent triadic integration
```

## 13. Recursive character

The framework is recursive.

This means the same basic triadic pattern can appear at multiple scales.

The basic Layer 0 pattern is:

```text
★ Quality → ● Contact → ■ Continuity → ★–●–■ Triadic Integration
```

This pattern can be used to examine:

- a moment of perception
- a conversation
- a habit
- a relationship
- a project
- a creative practice
- a decision
- a life pattern
- a conceptual framework
- an AI reasoning process

The point is not that all situations are identical.

The point is that many situations can be examined through a repeated structural question:

```text
What is the Quality?
What is the Contact?
What is the Continuity?
What kind of Triadic Integration is forming?
```

## 14. Relationship to Layer 1

Layer 1 translates the structural grammar of Layer 0 into mechanical systems.

Layer 0 asks:

```text
What are the basic structural ingredients?
```

Layer 1 asks:

```text
How do those ingredients behave as a working system?
```

Layer 1 develops three mechanical systems:

- **⟶ The Three Forces**
- **⬡ The Three Capacities**
- **↻ The Three Operations**

Layer 0 is the structural grammar.

Layer 1 is the mechanical model.

---

## 15. Relationship to Layer 2

Layer 2 is Recursive System Scales.

Layer 0 asks:

```text
What is the structure?
```

Layer 1 asks:

```text
How does the structure work mechanically?
```

Layer 2 asks:

```text
At what scale is this structure operating — Agent, Relation, Field, or their relational combinations?
```

The seven Layer 2 scales are:

- ○ Agent Scale
- ○ Relation Scale
- ○ Field Scale
- ⟷ Agent–Relation Scale
- ⟷ Relation–Field Scale
- ⟷ Agent–Field Scale
- △ Recursive System Ecology

Layer 0 primitives (Quality, Contact, Continuity) operate at every scale. Layer 2 locates *which* scale is relevant to a given situation.

Human-facing translations of the framework belong to the **Applied Framework** as Registers, not to Layer 2.

---

## 16. Relationship to diagnostics

Layer 0 is not diagnostic.

The diagnostic overlay belongs primarily to Layer 1 and practical use.

The canonical diagnostic distortions are:

- **∿ Slack**
- **≡ Tight**
- **↯ Misdirected**

The canonical well-formed condition is:

- **✓ Coherent Formation**

These terms should not be projected backward onto Layer 0 as if the primitives themselves are healthy, unhealthy, good, or bad.

Layer 0 describes structural variation.

Layer 1 can interpret those variations as practical patterns of stuckness, distortion, or coherent formation. Applied Framework Registers translate these into human-facing language for specific domains.

## 17. Intellectual background note

The framework has loose resonance with triadic traditions of thought, including Peircean themes such as quality, encounter, and continuity.

This is background resonance, not a formal derivation.

This repository is not a Peirce scholarship project.

Preferred wording:

```text
The framework has loose resonance with Peircean themes such as quality, encounter, and continuity.
```

Avoid stronger claims such as:

```text
The framework is derived from Peirce.
The framework is Peircean in structure.
The framework proves Peirce’s categories.
```

Layer 0 should stand or fall by whether its distinctions help clarify experience, not by appeal to an external authority.

## 18. Human-and-agent-readable use

Layer 0 is intended to be readable by both humans and AI agents.

A person or AI agent can ask:

```text
What is salient here?        → ★ Quality
What is being encountered?   → ● Contact
What pattern is continuing?  → ■ Continuity
What relation is forming?    → ★–●–■ Triadic Integration
```

This can support:

- reflective writing
- practical decision-making
- diagnostic routing
- agent prompts
- self-coaching tools
- comparison across frameworks
- structured interpretation of experience

Layer 0 does not produce answers by itself.

It provides the structural grammar that later layers and expression formats can use.

## 20. Minimum viable definition

The shortest useful definition of Layer 0 is:

> Layer 0: The Structural Layer describes experience as a recurring relation among **★ Quality**, **● Contact**, and **■ Continuity**. These three primitives generate three dyads, and the dyads generate **★–●–■ Triadic Integration**: the emergent coherence of a formed triadic relation.

---

## 21. Working rule

When developing any new part of the framework, check whether it can be traced back to Layer 0.

Ask:

```text
Where is ★ Quality?
Where is ● Contact?
Where is ■ Continuity?
What dyads are active?
What kind of Triadic Integration is forming?
```

If a new concept cannot be traced back to this structure, it may still be interesting, but it may not belong in the core framework.
